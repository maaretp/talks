# Testing Insights for Agentic Era

_Length: 1 day or four 1.5 hrs sessions_

**Description**

We already see the shift with agentic AI: more pull requests, larger pull request and misses in empirical evaluation become more production issues. The quality filter needs all hands on deck, and testing is too important to be left just for testers, but also too important not to be supported with multiple perspectives.

In this training, we acquire exploratory testing insights for the agentic era through experiential learning. Each of the four themed sessions comes with a hands on test target leading us to the necessary perspectives.

All the exercises and materials are available publicly under Creative Commons Attribution Non-Commercial Share-alike license. The core of the training is in the facilitation and delivery of insights, founded on the extensive experience of the instructor.

**Sessions**

Session 1: Good enough quality and importance of knowing domain

In this session, we test a small application with a list of known bugs, using AI tools. We set the baseline of results we are able to provide out of testing, and address the fact that all information isn't equal.

- exercise: the eprimer - domain knowledge and setting the bar for results
- insights:
  - Results baseline from "testers" 16% to "agents" 55% to "better testers with agents" 100%
  - Human-centric quality decisions: importance of agency, good enough quality, the relative rule
  - The Jevons paradox: when the efficiency of doing something increases, demand for it rises.

Session 2: Complementing useful requirements

In this session, we work from a requirement to enforce great specifications as useful, rather than complete. We'll address socializing and history-keeping perspectives of our documentation, tying it together with context-engineering.

- exercise: the atm - 300€ daily withdrawal limit
- insights:
  - Application and AI as external imagination
  - Reference, inference, conference
  - Human-in-the-loop, investing into being responsible while orchestrating

Session 3: Testing on two levels of quality

In this session, we level up our expectations of quality by all of us being on someone else's software with full control over improving it. The more and faster we deliver, the more the potholes in the road make us wobble.

- exercise: the potion shop - version 1 with bugs, version 2 without bugs, agentic fixing in between
- insights:
  - the minefield metaphor - known bugs hit us harder with agentic era
  - locking an accepted history while world around us changes
  - task expansion, 2nd pair of eyes, maintaining critical distance

Session 4: Architecture-awareness and lifecycle view

In this session, we take a three tier architecture of control points of exploring with automation. We address why details of tool selections matter for the deterministic controls we build for repeatability and control of agentic work.

- exercise: the rabbit - layered architecture for risk-based targeted tests
- insights:
  - architecture-aware testing
  - automated test maintenance
  - cost of quality beyond appraisal
